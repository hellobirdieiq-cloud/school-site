# Common Nouns and Proper Nouns Quiz

**Name:** ____________________ **Date:** ____________

## Rules

- A **common noun** names *any* person, place, thing, or idea. It is **not** capitalized. (girl, city, bridge, holiday)
- A **proper noun** names *one specific* person, place, thing, or idea. It **is** capitalized. (Anne Frank, Salt Lake City, Golden Gate Bridge, Hanukkah)
- Tip: Try putting **a** or **an** in front of the noun. If it sounds wrong ("a San Francisco"), it is probably a proper noun.
- Compound nouns count as ONE noun. Underline all the words. (food court, solar system, self-confidence)

## Directions

Underline each **common noun once**. Underline each **proper noun twice**.
